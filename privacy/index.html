Privacy

Privacy

How this portfolio handles information you choose to share.

Browsing

This static portfolio has no visitor accounts, application database or application analytics. The hosting provider may process request information to serve and secure the site.

Contact messages

When the contact form is enabled, your name, email, inquiry type, optional company and message pass directly to Web3Forms and the receiving inbox. The public form identifier identifies the recipient configuration. Provider acceptance does not guarantee inbox delivery. The portfolio does not store messages in an application database.

Spam protection

The enabled form loads hCaptcha to check submissions for abuse. That external service may process technical and interaction information under its own policies. No captcha loads while this portfolio’s form is disabled. Direct email remains available.

Retention and your choices

Message retention depends on the form provider and receiving inbox. Exact account retention settings must be verified before form activation; this site does not promise immediate deletion or zero provider storage. Contact the portfolio owner to ask about messages you have sent.

External services

Source, social and demo links open external sites with their own privacy practices. Read the service policies below for more information.

  • hCaptcha privacy
  • GitHub privacy
